Theme 02 · Freight Cycle

Capacity Tightens While Demand Returns; Pricing Power Reawakens

Submission: HIGHPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

Four signals printed on the same day point at the same inflection. Cass reported the smallest year-over-year freight volume decline in 18 months and explicitly called for a second-half recovery, ending what FreightWaves described as 40 consecutive months of negative comps. Routing guides are breaking down faster than at any point this cycle, with tender rejections surging as carriers walk away from contract rates negotiated when the market was softer. Transport Topics documented 28,000 non-domiciled CDLs already revoked under FMCSA's enforcement push, with roughly 200,000 more drivers expected to exit the workforce as the rule fully bites. And LRT Group's acquisition of F2F Transport is the kind of consolidation move that historically marks a cycle bottom. Pricing power is returning to carriers at the same moment shippers' incumbent contract structures fail, which is the textbook setup for a wave of re-brokered insurance placements and rising premium per unit.

CW Action

Accelerate the Q2 surge messaging tied to the OTRI capacity-tightening signal because these stories are the proof points, and push National Partners to lean into a 'time to re-broker the book' pitch ahead of Q3 renewals. Re-prioritize FMCSA new-venture targeting to capture displaced owner-operators rebuilding authorities under the new compliance regime; they are clean DriveSmart candidates by definition. This is also the right moment to ask the five strategic partners to surface incumbent placements where the carrier appears under-staffed for the safety-data conversation we can now lead.
