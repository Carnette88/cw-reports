Capacity Is Tightening — Submission Tailwind Building

Submission: HIGHPricing: MEDDistribution: LOW

Why It Matters

Five independent signals are converging on the same conclusion: capacity is tightening into Q2. OTRI hit 20.33% (in the 'tightening' band) and is rising +4.59% MoM. OTVI demand is up +12.07% MoM. Class 8 truck orders jumped 201% in April. Cass linehaul rates surged the same month. Spot rates set a record run during the CVSA Roadcheck blitz this week — even with 28,000 CDL revocations not yet showing up in pricing, suggesting the market is primed but not yet at peak. Historically this is the environment that drives operators to shop insurance — they're earning more, hauling more, and rotating into more equipment. The Q2 surge strategy we built around the OTRI signal is materializing as modeled.

CW Action

Tie this signal directly to the Q2 surge levers — Carrier Access push, FMCSA new-venture targeting, and the API partner activation work. If OTRI sustains above 20% for another two weeks, that's the cue to lean in harder on the operational levers.
