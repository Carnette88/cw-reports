Theme 03 · Regulatory & Cost Pressure

Carrier Economics Are Being Squeezed From Every Side

Submission: MEDPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

Carriers are absorbing simultaneous cost pressure from five directions this week. The Fair Compensation for Truck Crash Victims Act was reintroduced — it would lift the federal financial responsibility minimum from $750K to $5M, a step smaller MGAs and regional carriers can't follow cleanly. ATA-championed CORCA cleared the House to address $18M/day in cargo theft losses. CVSA International Roadcheck wrapped today with ELD-cheat enforcement now outpacing the cheaters. New Jersey introduced new truck parking, GPS, and overweight rules. Maverick just raised flatbed driver pay $0.04/mi, signaling tight labor. Net effect for our prospects: rising compliance + insurance + labor + security costs all at once. The carriers who survive this are the ones with continuous underwriting and real safety data — the core of our pitch.

CW Action

Re-frame the DriveSmart pitch for the next 90 days as a cost-control story, not just a safety story. The $5M minimum bill in particular is a strategic positive — if it advances, our admitted + E&S structure becomes a hard moat.
