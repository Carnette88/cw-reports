Roadcheck Just Pulled Capacity — Spot Rates Already Responding

Submission: HIGHPricing: MEDDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

The annual CVSA Roadcheck blitz this week parked more than 500 trucks in a single day on brakes alone, Oregon DOT put 283 trucks out of service for ELD violations, and FMCSA pulled another dozen ELDs from the registry — three concurrent capacity removals on top of the $217M federal push to drive bad actors out of CDL eligibility. Overdrive is reporting spot rates hit new highs on the back of the blitz, the cleanest real-time confirmation of OTRI-style tightening you'll find. ATA's April tonnage index landed flat, which means demand is steady while supply just got materially smaller — exactly the cyclical setup that drives carriers to re-shop insurance. Every unit removed from the registry is a fleet looking for coverage to keep its replacement truck moving.

CW Action

This is precisely the Q2 surge scenario the OTRI capacity-tightening signal was built for — pull forward any planned API Partner activations and Carrier Access audit follow-ups so we're positioned to absorb the submission lift. Have BD pivot talk tracks toward FMCSA new-venture targets and small fleets whose ELDs were just decertified, because they need a fast bind. The 17,492/month baseline should be tracked daily this week against the Roadcheck-driven inflection.
