Capacity Whiplash — 2026 Build Slots Filling, Spot Rates Cross Contract for First Time Since 2022

Submission: HIGHPricing: MEDDistribution: HIGH

Why It Matters

The freight cycle has visibly turned in the last week. Preliminary June Class 8 net orders came in around 30,500 units, up 16% month-over-month and 241% year-over-year, and analysts now expect the remaining 2026 build slots to fill in July — shifting the market from an order-driven phase into pure capacity allocation. Dry van spot rates topped contract rates for the first time since 2022, with dry van at a record $3.10/mile and reefer at $3.71/mile. Daimler Truck North America is reversing 2025 layoffs at Mount Holly, rehiring 400 and adding 200 more, and July container imports are on pace to set a record. For a trucking insurance carrier this is the OTRI-style convergence we plan Q2 surge around: new authorities will be written faster, existing fleets will re-shop as economics improve, and MGAs will start pushing volume through every appointed channel they have.

CW Action

Green-light the Q2 surge playbook this week: pull the FMCSA new-venture list, get BD talking about DriveSmart's continuous-underwriting story on every partner call, and push National Partners and the five strategic partners for a submission target increase for August. Prioritize API-partner activation over any refactor work — every day we shave off partner onboarding directly converts capacity-tightening headlines into submission volume against the 17,492/month baseline.
