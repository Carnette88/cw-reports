The Cycle Has Turned — Capacity Tightens, Submission Window Opens

Submission: HIGHPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

Multiple independent signals converged this week pointing the same direction. Truckload carriers like GP Transco are raising driver pay by five cents a mile, Werner is reporting that dedicated contract rates are starting to climb, LTL carriers are seeing shipment weights tick up, and the Port of LA logged a 17 percent volume surge in May as importers frontload ahead of policy risk. At the same time, Sparhawk Trucking is shopping itself with mass-layoff warnings and Transport Topics is flagging that 58 percent of drivers are now looking for new seats — the classic top-of-cycle fingerprint where rising rates and turnover hit together. For a commercial trucking insurer this is the moment the submission pipe widens: fleets shopping coverage, owner-operators standing up new authorities, and displaced drivers landing at new carriers who all need policies in force. It also resets pricing power on renewals after eighteen months of soft trade-down behavior.

CW Action

Treat this as the OTRI Q2 surge thesis becoming fact, not forecast — accelerate Carrier Access throughput on FMCSA new-venture targeting and push National Partners and the Howden US Trucking Practice to lean into the submission window before the rest of the market repositions. BD talk tracks this week should pair the driver-pay and turnover data with the DriveSmart pitch, because every carrier raising pay is also exposed to less-experienced seat-fillers who score better on continuous underwriting than on static MVRs. Update the partner outreach cadence to prioritize fleets cycling drivers and standing up new lanes — they are this week's highest-intent shoppers.
