Theme 02 · Loss Costs & Litigation

Fraud and Verdicts Keep the Loss-Cost Line Climbing

Submission: MEDPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

The stories driving trucking insurance economics this weekend all point the same direction — up. Cargo theft and carrier-identity fraud dominated the trade press, with the TIA carrying cargo theft and double-brokering to Capitol Hill and fresh coverage of how shippers are scrambling to verify who is actually hauling their freight. At the same time, weekend safety reporting — a fatal truck-involved crash under investigation and an operator running bald tires without a valid CDL — captures exactly the fact pattern that fuels nuclear verdicts, which are running up roughly 52% with median awards north of $50 million. Commercial auto has now been unprofitable for insurers for well over a decade, and excess and umbrella layers continue to post double-digit increases. For Cover Whale, this is the structural tailwind under pricing: loss-cost inflation keeps the market hard, keeps standard carriers cautious, and keeps well-underwritten risks shopping for a home.

CW Action

Keep DriveSmart front and center as the answer to this exact problem — telematics-based continuous underwriting is how we identify and price away the bald-tire, unqualified-driver profiles that produce nuclear verdicts. Arm BD personnel with a loss-cost talk track that ties our safety-tech story directly to the fraud and verdict headlines a fleet owner is already reading. On accounts where standard markets are pulling back, our admitted and E&S mix lets us stay on risk where others exit.
