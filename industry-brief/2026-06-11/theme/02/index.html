Theme 02 · Freight Flow & Distribution

Trade Plumbing Reshuffles — USMCA Wobble, Amazon's LTL Lunge, Tariff Whiplash

Submission: MEDPricing: MEDDistribution: HIGH

Why It Matters

Three stories landed within hours of each other on Tuesday. The administration signaled it will not renew USMCA, opening months of cross-border uncertainty for cross-border carriers. Treasury data showed tariff refunds in May nearly matched collected duties, suggesting the trade-policy machine is generating as much chaos as revenue. Amazon then formalized its less-than-truckload service for all shippers, while ArcBest/ABF announced a 5.9% general rate increase — meaning the LTL market is simultaneously absorbing a giant new entrant and pushing rates higher. For a commercial trucking carrier, this is not a single narrative — it is two: cross-border for-hire submission flow is about to get noisier, and the LTL competitive map is being redrawn under our feet.

CW Action

Pull a list of bound accounts with material US-Mexico or US-Canada exposure and have the BD team run a 90-day check-in cycle — USMCA uncertainty is the kind of event that moves shopping behavior even when nothing changes legally. Ask the LTL-adjacent retail vertical leads how Amazon's broader LTL availability is showing up in their shipper conversations and whether it changes the small-fleet appetite story we're hearing. The Howden US Trucking Practice appointment is the right channel to source cross-border submission tailwind from — formalize a Q3 push there.
