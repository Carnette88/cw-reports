Carrier Economics Squeezed From Every Side

Submission: HIGHPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

Fuel has retaken the top spot on ATRI's annual list of trucking's most pressing concerns, with the driver shortage climbing back into the top five and insurance still lodged among the squeeze. Crude touched $110 for the first time since May and California diesel sits above $8, pushing record fuel surcharges through to shippers even as underlying rates stay soft. J.B. Hunt's dedicated division posted a 22% year-over-year drop in operating income and pointed explicitly at higher driver expenses, medical claims, and insurance premiums as the culprits. For a trucking insurer this is a double signal: input-cost inflation thins carrier margins and accelerates insurance shopping, while the premium line item that carriers now openly blame for lost profit confirms the hard market has room to run. When operators feel the pinch from every direction, they re-shop coverage and scrutinize price-to-value harder than in any soft stretch.

CW Action

Treat this cost squeeze as submission fuel and lean into the value story the DriveSmart telematics program lets us tell. Arm the BD team with a talk track that reframes premium as a controllable line item through continuous underwriting and safety-driven discounts, directly answering the carriers who are blaming insurance for eroded margins. Prioritize re-shopping conversations with FMCSA new-venture and cost-pressured mid-market accounts where the economics are tightest right now.
