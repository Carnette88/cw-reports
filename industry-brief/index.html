Today in 30 seconds

Three forces are pulling on the trucking insurance market today: capacity is tightening even as freight tonnage slips, federal enforcement is being rewired around brokers and ELDs after the Montgomery ruling, and cargo theft plus AI-enabled fraud are becoming a measurable line item in carrier loss ratios. Each one feeds a different part of Cover Whale's funnel — submission flow, distribution leverage, and underwriting selection — and all three are moving the same direction at once.
