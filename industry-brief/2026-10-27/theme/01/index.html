Theme 01 · Freight Cycle

The Recovery Is Real — Capacity Tightens, Pricing Power Returns

Submission: HIGHPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

The freight recovery has moved from forecast to financial statement. Marten Transport's Q3 profit rose 31% on higher truckload rates, mirroring the capacity-driven margin rebound carriers across the sector reported this earnings season, and September trailer orders surged 311% year over year as fleets finally re-equip. At the same time, ATA tonnage data shows the rebound is uneven and cash-constrained — commercial carriers are hauling more loads while liquidity tightens because operating costs land before customer payments do. For a trucking insurer, this convergence is the textbook setup for a submission wave: tightening capacity pulls marginal operators back into the market and firming rates change what fleets can afford, which resets insurance-shopping behavior. The dynamic directly feeds the OTRI capacity signal underpinning the Q2 surge strategy, and it means premium is hardening into a market where new and returning operators need coverage to take advantage of better freight.

CW Action

Treat this as the leading edge of the Q2 submission surge and pull the OTRI capacity narrative into BD talk tracks now so personnel can tie the freight recovery directly to a fleet's coverage decision. Arm the BD team with the Marten/trailer-order data points to open conversations with growth-minded carriers, and position DriveSmart's continuous underwriting as the way a cash-tight operator earns premium relief as it adds trucks rather than waiting for renewal. This is the moment to push toward the double-submissions OKR while shopping behavior is accelerating.
