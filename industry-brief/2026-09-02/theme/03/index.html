Theme 03 · Freight Cycle

Bottom of the Cycle: Soft Demand, Failing Carriers, Attriting Capacity

Submission: HIGHPricing: MEDDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

The demand side is not cooperating with the cost side. Manufacturing growth slowed again in August per the latest ISM print, spot rates are falling even as diesel spikes, leaving carrier costs and revenue disconnected in the worst possible way. The attrition is visible everywhere: freight-tech platform TruckSmarter is shutting down and stranding hundreds of thousands of carriers, Yellow's estate reached $526 million in pension settlements, and drayage operators are pulling outsourcing in-house amid a driver shortage. A prolonged freight-recession bottom is the classic setup for a submission surge, distressed carriers shop aggressively, weak ones exit, and survivors restructure fleets and coverage. It is also the setup for a capacity-tightening inflection, which is exactly the OTRI signal the Q2 surge strategy was built to catch as the market turns from soft to hardening.

CW Action

Treat this week's soft-demand-plus-carrier-failure signals as the leading edge of the capacity-tightening thesis and accelerate API partner activation so we can absorb submission volume when distressed carriers move. Direct National Partners, Networks, and the five strategic partners to lean into re-shopping accounts now, and keep the Carrier Access audit on schedule so top-of-funnel capacity is ready to defend the double-submissions OKR when the cycle turns.
