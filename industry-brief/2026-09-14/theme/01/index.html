Freight Fraud Goes Federal — And It's a Loss-Cost Story

Submission: MEDPricing: HIGHDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

Cargo theft, carrier impersonation, staged loads, falsified ELD records and trailer scams have shifted from nuisance to a systemic driver of both cargo and liability losses, and this week the pattern surfaced across every major feed at once. The $586K copper-load theft via carrier impersonation and the growing scrutiny of ghost-driver ELD falsification show organized fraud embedding itself in the freight transaction, while DOT Secretary Duffy publicly framing the removal of fraudsters as policy signals a regulatory tailwind for operators that can prove clean books. For an insurer this hardens the loss-cost narrative that underpins the current premium environment, and it rewards carriers whose vetting and telematics can separate legitimate risks from bad actors before they enter the book. That capability is precisely where Cover Whale's DriveSmart telematics, Carrier Access audit and FMCSA new-venture screening create underwriting advantage. The convergence of enforcement rhetoric and real theft events gives the market fresh justification to hold or push rate.

CW Action

Turn vetting into a BD talking point this week: position DriveSmart and the Carrier Access audit as fraud-resistant underwriting that disciplined, legitimate carriers benefit from rather than a hurdle. Brief National Partners and Networks that federal enforcement is validating rigorous selection, and use the impersonation and ELD-fraud headlines in renewal conversations to defend rate. Lean into FMCSA new-venture targeting where clean telematics data separates good risks early.
