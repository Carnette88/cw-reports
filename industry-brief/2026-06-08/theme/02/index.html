Theme 02 · Capacity Structure

Capacity Math Tightens From Every Side — Trailers, Drivers, Autonomy, Leadership All Moving

Submission: HIGHPricing: MEDDistribution: MED

Why It Matters

Five separate signals this week point to the same conclusion: the cost and supply structure of trucking capacity is being reset, and not in a single direction. Commerce is backing duties on Chinese and Mexican van trailers, which raises replacement-fleet costs into 2027. The May jobs report came in at 172,000 — a softer print that suggests freight demand isn't bouncing back fast enough to absorb capacity coming offline. Texas is moving to English-only CDL exams, narrowing the driver pipeline at the bottom while more states bet legislatively on driverless trucks at the top. And the retirement of Knight-Swift's Kevin Knight closes a generational chapter in fleet leadership at the precise moment the industry is being asked to reorganize. Each signal on its own is moderate; together they push the smart-money read toward capacity tightening and submission flow accelerating in Q3 and Q4 as carriers shop and renew under uncertainty.

CW Action

Lean into the OTRI capacity-tightening narrative our Q2 surge strategy was built around — these are the confirming data points. Have BD raise it explicitly with the five strategic partners and the Howden US Trucking Practice this week. Internally, make sure underwriting has a clear position on Mexico/Canada-domiciled equipment and on driver-source verification so we can quote confidently as the market shifts under us.
