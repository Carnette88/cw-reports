Theme 01 · Freight Cycle

The Freight Floor Is Firming — Pricing Power Is Creeping Back

Submission: HIGHPricing: MEDDistribution: LOW

Why It Matters

A cluster of demand signals turned positive in the same 48 hours, and they reinforce rather than contradict each other. U.S. factory production posted its strongest growth since 2021 and manufacturing expanded for a tenth straight month, the Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach set all-time volume records, and Old Dominion says shippers are now asking for multiyear contracts to lock rates before they climb — a tell that the demand side expects capacity to tighten. XPO's 12% revenue gain on higher-margin freight shows the stronger carriers are already converting that firmer demand into price. For a trucking insurer this is the leading edge of the cycle that drives submissions: as freight demand recovers, new authorities form, fleets re-equip, and shopping activity accelerates. The convergence of production, port, and contract-behavior signals is exactly the capacity-tightening setup Cover Whale's Q2 surge thesis is built to catch, and it is showing up earlier than a normal seasonal read would suggest.

CW Action

Treat this as confirmation to lean into the Q2 surge plan rather than wait for it — pull forward the OTRI capacity-tightening talk track and arm the BD team with the manufacturing-and-port demand data as a 'the market is turning, lock your appetite now' message to partners. Prioritize FMCSA new-venture targeting while authority formation is accelerating, since the earliest submissions in a firming cycle convert best. Make sure the newly appointed Howden US Trucking practice is briefed on the demand read so their first placements ride the tailwind.
